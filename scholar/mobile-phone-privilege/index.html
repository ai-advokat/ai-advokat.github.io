Abstract

Searching a mobile phone may expose legal advice, defence strategy and the confidential records of several clients. Lawful seizure of the device does not, by itself, justify unrestricted examination of its contents. The paper examines the relationship between powers to obtain electronic evidence and the protection of legal professional privilege in North Macedonian law. Particular attention is given to a client’s phone, a lawyer’s phone, mixed-content devices, cloud access and suspicion of criminal involvement by a lawyer. Its principal conclusion is that protection must operate before confidential material becomes available to the prosecution team. The proposed model combines sealed preservation, limited technical acquisition, independent filtering and judicial determination of disputed confidentiality.
