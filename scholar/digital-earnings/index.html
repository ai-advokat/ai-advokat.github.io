Abstract

This preprint examines the taxation of income earned through digital platforms by individuals resident in North Macedonia. It distinguishes income classification, the tax base, foreign withholding and the domestic foreign tax credit, with particular attention to electronic service, limitation periods and legal certainty. The study combines doctrinal analysis, selected scholarly literature and a limited comparison with Serbia and international platform-reporting frameworks. Hypothetical examples and nine practical appendices illustrate an evidential protocol linking platform statements, bank receipts and the procedural case file.

The legal analysis has a reference date of 21 October 2026. The paper and its English translation were approved by the author on 22 October 2026. This preprint has not undergone journal peer review.
