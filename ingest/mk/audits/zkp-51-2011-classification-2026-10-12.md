# ZKP 51/2011 lineage resolution — 2026-10-12

Status: **51/2011 BLOCKER RESOLVED / CURRENT-LAW PROMOTION STILL BLOCKED**

## Finding

Official Gazette 51/2011, item 1381, does **not** amend the new Criminal Procedure Act 150/2010.

Article 1 of the 51/2011 act expressly targets the predecessor Criminal Procedure Act lineage:
15/1997, 44/2002, 74/2004, 83/2008 and 67/2009.

The act changes predecessor-law Articles 142-б, 142-г, 200 and 202.

Its Article 5 states that the provisions of the 51/2011 amending act cease when the 150/2010 Criminal Procedure Act begins application.

## Link to 100/2012

Official Gazette 100/2012, item 2624, amends the 150/2010 Act itself.

It:
- adds 51/2011 to the predecessor-law references in Articles 566 and 567; and
- replaces the original delayed-application wording in Article 568 with application from **1 December 2013**.

Therefore 51/2011 belongs to the predecessor/transitional chain, not the normative amendment lineage of the 570-article 2010 Act.

## Correct lineage model

Current/new-law normative lineage:
150/2010 → 100/2012 → 142/2016 → Constitutional Court 193/2016 → 198/2018.

Predecessor/transitional lineage:
15/1997 → 44/2002 → 74/2004 → 83/2008 → 67/2009 → 51/2011.

The repository's secondary 570-article snapshot was therefore correct **not to include 51/2011 in its normative source label**.

## Timing correction

- 150/2010 entered into force: 26 November 2010.
- Its original application rule was later replaced by 100/2012.
- Full application of the new Act begins: **1 December 2013**.
- The 51/2011 predecessor amendment ceases generally with that application trigger, subject to the transitional treatment of proceedings already begun under Articles 566-567.

## Remaining gates

This resolves only the 51/2011 classification blocker. It does not make the 570-article secondary consolidation authoritative current law.
