# ZKP official-lineage audit — 2026-10-12

Status: **51/2011 CLASSIFIED / ARTICLE-LEVEL OFFICIAL RECONSTRUCTION STILL REQUIRED**

## Official status

LDBIS identifies the 2010 Criminal Procedure Act as active.

The base Act 150/2010 was published on 18 November 2010 and entered into force on 26 November 2010. Its original delayed-application rule was later changed by Official Gazette 100/2012 so that the new Act began application on **1 December 2013**.

## 51/2011 classification resolved

The exact official Gazette 51/2011 act (item 1381) targets the predecessor Criminal Procedure Act:
15/1997, 44/2002, 74/2004, 83/2008 and 67/2009.

It does not directly amend the text of the new 150/2010 Act.

Its own Article 5 provides that its provisions cease with the start of application of the 150/2010 Act. Official Gazette 100/2012 then amends Articles 566-567 of the new Act to include 51/2011 in the predecessor-law transition chain and changes Article 568 so the new Act begins application on 1 December 2013.

Therefore:

- **new/current normative lineage:** 150/2010 → 100/2012 → 142/2016 → CC 193/2016 → 198/2018;
- **predecessor/transitional lineage:** 15/1997 → 44/2002 → 74/2004 → 83/2008 → 67/2009 → 51/2011.

The repository's 570-article secondary reference snapshot is not deficient merely because its normative label omits 51/2011.
