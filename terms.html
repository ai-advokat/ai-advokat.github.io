Terms of Use

Research assistance, source verification and Human Gate rules.

Important: AI Advokat is a legal-information and research-assistance portal. Use of the portal does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship.

1. Research assistance

Search results, retrieval packets, citation checks and document skeletons are research aids. They are not a substitute for checking the current legal text, procedural rules, primary sources or professional legal judgment.

2. Human Gate

Any legal conclusion, filing, advice, publication or professional use requires human verification and approval. AI output or automated retrieval must not be treated as final authority.

3. Source status

The portal distinguishes official, verified, pending and draft material. A reserved DOI for an unpublished Zenodo draft is not represented as a published record.

4. Confidential data

Do not submit confidential client data to public forms. Secure upload and case workspaces are intentionally disabled until the required safeguards are implemented.

5. External links

External official and research services are provided for navigation and verification. AI Advokat does not control third-party availability or content.

6. No automated outcome promise

The portal does not promise litigation outcomes and does not provide probability-of-success scores as legal conclusions.
