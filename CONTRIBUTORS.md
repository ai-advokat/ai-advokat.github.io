## Verification principles

1. Attribute specific technical changes using contemporaneous evidence when available; do not infer authorship solely from a GitHub account or an AI-generated file header.
2. Keep source-code licences, third-party dependencies and potential intellectual-property assignments distinct from narrative acknowledgements.
3. Do not turn contributor acknowledgement into a claim of equity, co-ownership, employment or profit entitlement.
4. Make additions or corrections through dated commits. Never rewrite source history to alter credit.
5. Legal advice and the decision to publish any personal or financial details belong to the human project participants.

## Commercial matters

Any proposed private revenue- or profit-sharing arrangement is intentionally excluded from this public repository. Relevant parties should document it separately in a signed agreement, including definitions, accounting, intellectual-property rights, decision-making and exit procedures.
