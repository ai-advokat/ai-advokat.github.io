## Operational rules used by AI Advokat

1. Standard literary Macedonian and Macedonian Cyrillic are the default for Macedonian-facing work.
2. Ordinary titles and ranks use lower-case initials unless another capitalisation rule applies.
3. Common legal nouns before a concrete number remain lower-case, e.g. “член 5”, “став 1”.
4. Ordinary abbreviations normally use capital letters without full stops; source-faithful official identifiers are preserved verbatim.
5. Punctuation and spacing are normalised in generated prose, while quotations and source-faithful spans remain untouched.
6. Formal headings use clean document formatting and do not end in a full stop unless the document form requires otherwise.
7. Bibliographic references must map clearly to their bibliographic descriptions; citation format never upgrades secondary literature into legal authority.
8. Respect forms and correspondence salutations follow the Macedonian orthographic contract already encoded in the runtime.
