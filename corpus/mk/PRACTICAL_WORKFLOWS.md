# AI Advokat Practical Legal Workflows


### 9. Violence in public transport with a possible ethnic / hate motive
- immediate safety and medical documentation
- exact location, route, vehicle and time
- CCTV preservation request from bus/operator/nearby systems where legally available
- driver, passenger and other witness identification
- adult / juvenile status of every participant
- individual conduct of each participant; never assign collective responsibility
- weapons or dangerous objects, if any, recorded as evidence
- hate speech, slurs, symbols, prior threats or targeting recorded only when supported by evidence
- distinguish ordinary assault/group violence from a bias- or hate-motivated offence only after legal and factual verification
- preserve electronic evidence, messages and public posts lawfully
- victim-support and interpreter access where needed
- anti-discrimination / hate-crime legal analysis where applicable
- public-transport operator obligations and incident records where legally relevant
- strict privacy protection for minors and victims
- no ethnic profiling, stereotyping or inference from names, appearance, neighbourhood or group membership
- Human Gate before classifying motive or generating a filing


### 11. Legalization of unauthorized construction / легализација на бесправно изградени објекти
- identify the exact object, parcel and municipality
- determine construction/use date and whether the object falls within any legally eligible category
- verify the currently applicable legalization / treatment regime before generating any advice
- cadastral parcel and ownership/title checks
- geodetic report / survey documentation where legally required
- architectural/technical documentation where required
- municipal planning status and zoning constraints
- public/state land issues
- co-ownership and consent issues
- utility/infrastructure constraints
- protected area, cultural heritage, environmental or safety restrictions
- prior inspection, demolition, removal or enforcement decisions
- pending administrative or judicial proceedings
- fees/compensation only from a verified current official tariff or legal source
- deadlines must be taken from the current law or official decision and never guessed
- distinguish legalization from building permit, reconstruction approval, cadastral registration and ownership registration
- preserve all municipal decisions, notices, receipts and submission proofs
- Human Gate before filing, appeal, objection or representation

#### Draft outputs
- eligibility checklist
- document checklist
- request/petition where a valid legal route exists
- response to municipal request for supplementation
- objection / appeal against an administrative decision
- chronology of administrative steps
- evidence matrix
- client intake summary

#### Safety / integrity rules
- do not fabricate construction dates, ownership evidence, cadastral data or technical documents
- do not advise concealment of later construction or alteration
- do not treat an object as legal merely because it is registered in cadastre
- if no current legalization route exists, explain the lawful alternatives instead of inventing one
