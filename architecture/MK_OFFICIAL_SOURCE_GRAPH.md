# North Macedonia Official Source Graph

Version: 2026-09-29

## Authority hierarchy
1. Official Gazette + LDBIS current/version metadata
2. Courts and official judicial publications
3. Ministries, public funds, registries and professional chambers
4. Official service/verification portals
5. Regulatory monitors
6. Secondary/commercial platforms for discovery/benchmarking only

## Primary legal spine
**Official Gazette** supplies publication provenance.
**LDBIS** supplies high-value version/status metadata and public legal retrieval.

LDBIS covers laws, bylaws, other acts, case-law references and professional literature. A text is not treated as current merely because it is found; version, entry into force, application date and later amendments must be resolved.

## Justice hub
**Ministry of Justice** routes:
- LDBIS
- execution
- mediation
- notariat
- free legal aid
- judicial translators
- international legal instruments
- international legal assistance
- legal-aid lawyers
- experts
- licensed mediators
- legal clinics

## Judicial layer
**Judicial Portal** is the central discovery spine for:
- courts
- decisions
- publications
- professional works
- bulletins
- collections
- forms
- reports/statistics
- calendars

Authority-bearing court decisions are kept separate from context-only news/press releases.
e-Delivery is excluded.

## State representation
**State Attorney Office**: public laws, decisions, rulebooks, reports and institutional material.

## Corporate/secured-transactions layer
**Central Registry**: company/status verification, beneficial-owner workflows, pledges, leasing, fiduciary/retention-of-title services, liquidation/bankruptcy routing.
Published reuse restrictions mean no bulk mirroring of registry subject data without permission.

## Government services
**Uslugi.gov.mk** maps:
life event -> institution -> service -> prerequisites -> documents -> route.
eID/profile/private submissions are excluded.

## Tax
**Public Revenue Office (UJP)**:
- tax regulation
- guides
- forms
- tax calendar
- publications/FAQs
- public notices relevant to tax law
Taxpayer-specific/eTax data are excluded.

## Property
**Cadastre**:
- public regulations/documents
- property services
- permitted public registers/geospatial outputs
- property-sheet/history workflows
- price/rent outputs where permitted
Professional/authenticated systems are not mirrored.

## Professional justice bodies
**Chamber of Enforcement Agents**: public regulation, tariff references, directory verification, public procedural materials.
**Notary Chamber**: regulation, tariff, ethics, publications, directory verification.
**Bar Association**: acts, official lawyer lists/directories, training/professional resources and case-law references.

## Economy and labour
**Ministry of Economy and Labour**:
- labour law
- trade/company/business regulation
- insolvency/liquidation
- consumer law
- technical/product safety
- permits/licences
- public enterprises
- investment support
- misdemeanour commissions
- occupational safety and labour-market materials

Important: pages may include both enacted laws and draft laws. A proposal/draft is never classified as law in force until enacted and published in the Official Gazette.

## Health insurance
**FZO** gives excellent sectoral version signals:
- IN FORCE labels
- consolidated/purified text where published
- full amendment chain by Gazette issue
- Constitutional Court decisions linked to the statute
- procedures, medicines, forms and guidance
Controlling text is still cross-checked with Gazette/LDBIS.

## Business regulatory monitoring
**Economic Chamber** tracks ENER items, parliamentary bills and Gazette publications.
Use for change detection, not final authority.

## Secondary legal platforms
DeJure, Factum, PRAKSIS and PraVI:
- public feature benchmark
- public discovery/reference only
- no subscriber/proprietary corpus copying

## Global privacy/reuse gate
Never bulk-ingest:
- authenticated user/account data
- private court/e-delivery documents
- taxpayer-specific records
- private case files
- restricted Central Registry subject data
- personal data from enforcement notices/search results
- subscriber-only proprietary legal databases
