# Legal Market Intelligence Source Policy

Version: 2026-10-11
Scope: AI Advokat legal-market, provider-discovery and comparative-practice intelligence

## Core separation
Legal-market rankings, directories, editorial publications and practitioner guides are not sources of binding law.

They may support:
- law-firm and lawyer discovery;
- practice-area mapping;
- market and transaction trend analysis;
- jurisdiction/practice orientation;
- comparative issue spotting;
- identifying possible experts or service providers.

They may not independently support:
- current statutory text;
- controlling case-law holdings;
- procedural deadlines;
- jurisdictional conclusions;
- authoritative legal quotations.

Any substantive legal proposition discovered through these sources must be rebound to an official statute, court/tribunal decision, regulator, treaty source or other appropriate primary authority.

## Source classes

### Market-ranking / legal-intelligence platforms
Examples:
- Chambers and Partners;
- The Legal 500;
- IFLR / IFLR1000.

Use:
- provider discovery;
- market position and practice-area orientation;
- rankings as one research signal;
- public comparative guides as issue-spotting aids.

Rules:
- rankings are editorial/research products, not legal authority;
- rankings must never be presented as a guarantee of legal quality, outcome or suitability;
- ranking position must not silently override user requirements such as jurisdiction, language, fee model, conflict status or subject-matter fit;
- keep edition/year visible because ranking data is time-sensitive.

### Professional directories
Example:
- Martindale.

Use:
- provider identity and directory discovery only unless a richer public evidence surface is separately reviewed.

### Regional legal-market media
Example:
- CEE Legal Matters.

Use:
- regional market/news discovery only after article-level provenance and terms review.

## Comparative practice guides
Country/practice guides written by lawyers can be useful orientation.

Required label:
- secondary practitioner guidance.

Before using a guide for a material legal proposition:
1. resolve the jurisdiction and publication/update date;
2. identify the named author/firm if available;
3. verify the proposition against current official law;
4. preserve any known sponsored/editorial status.

## Editorial and sponsored content
Editorial news, awards coverage and sponsored articles are separate source classes.

Sponsored content must never be blended into independent editorial or legal authority.
A sponsored item can support:
- discovery of a topic or firm;
- issue spotting.

It cannot independently support:
- a legal conclusion;
- a provider-quality conclusion;
- an "independent ranking" claim.

## Commercial-content boundary
Unless source-specific terms are reviewed and permit more:
- no bulk scraping;
- no paywall bypass;
- no automated copying of commercial rankings/databases;
- no republishing copyrighted research text;
- store only minimal public metadata, source URL, edition/date and provenance.

## Provider recommendation rule
When AI Advokat helps locate counsel or firms:
- disclose that rankings/directories are secondary market signals;
- use multiple factors where available;
- do not infer conflicts, availability, fees, licensing status or current employment from a stale ranking;
- require live verification for current professional status before a concrete referral.

## Fail-closed rule
If only a logo, brand name or homepage identity has been supplied:
- create at most an identity-level source profile;
- do not infer product capabilities, ranking methodology, geographic coverage or legal-authority status beyond what the supplied material supports.
