# Access to Justice & Legal Provider Source Policy

Version: 2026-10-11
Scope: AI Advokat provider discovery, legal-aid navigation and access-to-justice information

## Core separation
Provider directories, legal-aid portals, NGOs and research guides are not substitutes for legislation, court decisions or official legal texts.

They may support:
- finding a lawyer or legal-aid provider;
- routing a user to an appropriate service;
- identifying areas of practice;
- locating public legal resources;
- explaining available service pathways.

They may not independently support:
- verified current law;
- binding eligibility conditions;
- procedural deadlines;
- authoritative holdings;
- guaranteed provider suitability, availability or outcome.

Material legal propositions must be rebound to current official law and appropriate primary authority.

## Source classes

### 1. Public legal-aid service portal
Use for:
- service navigation;
- provider categories;
- contact pathways;
- public explanations of primary/secondary legal aid.

Rule:
- eligibility criteria, statutory rights and procedural conditions must be verified against current law before consequential reliance.

### 2. NGO / civil-society legal-aid provider
Use for:
- legal-aid provider discovery;
- rights information;
- strategic-litigation/public-interest orientation;
- publications.

Rule:
- NGO publications remain secondary professional material unless they reproduce and precisely cite primary authority.

### 3. Private lawyer directory
Use for:
- provider discovery only after source-integrity review.

Rule:
- directory inclusion is not proof of bar status, specialization, availability, fee level, conflict clearance or quality;
- current professional status requires live verification;
- no automated referral activation without Human Gate.

### 4. Official research guide / legal-resource directory
Example: Law Library of Congress jurisdictional guide.

Use for:
- locating primary and secondary sources;
- understanding source hierarchy.

Rule:
- a research guide is not itself controlling law;
- inclusion of a source does not equal endorsement.

## Content-integrity quarantine
A source is quarantined when the supplied surface contains unrelated, suspicious or injected content that undermines trust in the page.

Quarantine consequences:
- no automated ingestion;
- no referral use;
- no provider activation;
- no legal-answer authority;
- no extraction of personal/provider records;
- manual re-verification required from a clean official/current surface.

## Current-provider verification
Before a concrete lawyer/provider referral, verify where reasonably available:
- current professional status / registration;
- current organization/employment;
- practice area;
- current contact channel;
- geographic/jurisdictional fit.

Never infer:
- conflicts of interest;
- fees;
- availability;
- success rate;
- quality guarantee.

## Personal-data minimisation
Provider discovery may use public professional metadata. Do not create bulk personal profiles or retain unnecessary personal identifiers.

## Human Gate
Provider activation or direct referral is a consequential action.
AI Advokat may suggest or rank discovery options, but provider activation/referral remains Human Gate controlled.

## Fail-closed rule
If source integrity, ownership or current status is uncertain:
- downgrade to discovery-only;
- label uncertainty;
- require verification;
- do not silently promote the source to official guidance or trusted referral.
