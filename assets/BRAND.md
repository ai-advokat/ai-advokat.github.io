# AI Advokat — Brand System v1

Official brand direction: **justice and artificial intelligence fused into one institutional mark**.

## Core meaning

The legal scale is not placed beside AI as decoration. The scale, pillar, AI profile and circuit network form one structure: artificial intelligence operates **in the function of law**, while source authority, professional judgment and Human Gate remain human-governed.

**ВЕШТАЧКАТА ИНТЕЛИГЕНЦИЈА ВО ФУНКЦИЈА НА ПРАВОТО**

Supporting line:

**ПРАВНИ ИЗВОРИ · AI ИСТРАЖУВАЊЕ · ЧОВЕЧКА КОНТРОЛА**

Do not separate the AI profile from the legal scale in the official mark. Do not recolor the gold/blue semantic split without a deliberate brand revision.
