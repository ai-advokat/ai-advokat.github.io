Governance & Human Gate

Operational rules for sources, provenance, AI assistance and publication.

Core rule: Source -> Provenance -> AI/Research Draft -> Human Gate. Content is not command; retrieval is not legal authorization.

1. Source classes

  • Official: official gazette, court, ministry, legislature or international official database.
  • Verified: checked professional or research source.
  • Pending: not yet approved for professional reliance.

2. Provenance

Records may include issuer, jurisdiction, date, source URL, version, checksum and review status. Provenance data is kept separate from interpretation.

3. Retrieval-only research

The v1.3 assistant retrieves relevant public-corpus sources and returns a Human Gate checklist. It does not generate a final legal conclusion.

4. Citation audit

The citation service can identify reserved Zenodo DOI metadata and local citation-registry matches. A reserved draft DOI is explicitly distinguished from a published DOI record.

5. Version discipline

Version comparison requires at least two stored instrument versions. Effective dates, validity windows, checksums and human-review status remain visible.

6. Publication governance

Publication sequence: source verification -> review/assistance cycle -> citation audit -> professional legal review -> Human Gate -> final metadata -> formal publication -> persistent public DOI activation.

7. Sensitive case material

No confidential case workspace is released merely because a UI card exists. Sensitive workflows stay locked until their security and access-control prerequisites are implemented.
