Privacy Notice

Public-portal privacy scope and limits.

Current scope: the public portal is designed for public legal information and author/publication metadata. Secure client-file upload and case workspaces are not enabled.

1. Data categories

  • Public author identity and publication metadata displayed on the portal.
  • Search terms and research questions sent to the AI Advokat Worker when a user uses D1 search or retrieval-only research.
  • Standard technical request data may be processed by the infrastructure providers serving the website and API.

2. Confidential information

Do not submit attorney-client privileged material, health information, identity documents, passwords, payment information, or other sensitive client data through the public portal. Secure upload remains disabled until authentication, access controls, secure storage, retention rules and auditing are implemented.

3. External services

The portal links to external sources such as ORCID, Zenodo, official legal databases, courts, HUDOC and EUR-Lex. Their own privacy terms apply when you leave AI Advokat.

4. Purpose limitation

Public-corpus search and retrieval are used to locate legal-information records and source metadata. The portal does not sell user data.

5. Future secure features

Before any authenticated case workspace or confidential-document upload is released, this notice must be updated to describe the actual storage, retention, access-control and deletion model.
